Simulation Notice & Practice Scope:

This AI mock interview platform provides automated simulation and developmental practice feedback. Performance scores, readiness metrics, and model answers do not guarantee employment, recommendation, or selection by {{ interview.target_company }} or any other entity.

Interview Protocol & Scoring

  • One Question at a Time: The AI interviewer presents exactly one question at a time. The subsequent question will be generated only after you submit your answer or elect to skip.
  • Answering Modalities: You may dictate answers via your microphone (Speech-to-Text) or type directly. Dictated transcripts can be freely edited before final submission.
  • Objective 100-Point Rubric: Each answer is judged on technical accuracy (or STAR structure for HR), reasoning, completeness, edge case mitigation, and depth.
  • Optional Camera Preview: Camera access is strictly voluntary for personal posture monitoring. Video is processed locally in your browser; no video is permanently stored on external servers. You can proceed without a camera at any time.
  • Ending the Interview: You can click "End Interview" at any time. Any answered questions will be scored and compiled into your final downloadable PDF report.
